How the cars are counted

Country totals start from the OICA 2025 world production table (96.4M vehicles). Medium and heavy trucks and buses (about 3.4M) are removed so only light vehicles remain: cars, SUVs, pickups and vans. Brands are counted where the vehicle is assembled, so a Kentucky-built Toyota parks in the United States lot.

A brand that would not fill half a mini car in a country is dropped, along with unattributed volume. The 1,000 cars are then shared among what remains, so one car ≈ vehicles.

Inside a country

An opened country is re-parked at 100 cars, or at its world count if that is larger (China keeps all of its cars), so smaller countries still show their models. The half-a-car rule applies again at that scale, to brands and then to models. Each 3D car takes the body style of its model: sedan, hatchback, SUV, MPV, van, pickup, kei car or light truck.

reported means the brand's volume in that country comes from an official body or the maker. estimated means it was split from a group total or rebuilt from plant output. Model splits are always estimates.
